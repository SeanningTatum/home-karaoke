Emoji Reactions

2026-07-17 · feature: emoji-reactions (feat-010) · reviewed · round 1 — all 4 decisions answered 2026-07-17: (1) sequence recap 3.5s → You're-up 5s, (2) no DO throttle in v1, (3) palette 👏 🔥 ❤️ 😭 🤩 🎉, (4) phone fly-up yes / recap TV-only. Party-end recap confirmed as future feature.

TL;DR

Guests tap emoji on their phones while someone sings; the reactions fly up the TV screen Google-Meet-style, and when the song ends the TV shows a short recap ("Alice got 47 reactions 🔥❤️👏") before the existing "You're up" card. Everything rides the existing WebSocket protocol and pure-reducer architecture — no new D1 tables, no new bindings; per-song tallies live in the Durable Object and reset on every song change.

Reactions are transient events, not snapshot state — they bypass the RoomSocketState snapshot merge entirely (handled like the existing room.closed early-return) and reach the UI as callbacks. The per-song tally lives inside the DO's RoomLiveState, mutated by a pure reducer and reset by the existing advanceToNext; the DO skips its SQLite persist for reaction messages so 50 taps ≠ 50 storage writes.

Settled by the architecture review (no click needed)

  • Tally location — inside RoomLiveState via pure reducer (unit-testable), reset by advanceToNext; DO skips SQLite persist for reaction messages. Rejected: wire-schema field (leaks into every broadcast), raw DO private field (untestable).
  • Recap emission — broadcastsForMessage gains (prev, next) states; emits reaction.recap before queue.updated/playback.updated, only when the song actually advanced and the tally is non-zero.
  • Input surface — phones only (host reacts via own phone at /join/:code); TV stays passive, per the feat-009 beta decision.
  • Sounds — TV reaction pop throttled to ≤1 per 700ms + one recap sting; both are new synth JingleSpecs respecting the existing mute.
  • Reduced motion — fly-up skipped entirely (decorative); recap card + aria-live regions still render (informational).
