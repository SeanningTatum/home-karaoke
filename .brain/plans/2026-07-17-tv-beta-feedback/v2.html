TV Beta Feedback Polish

2026-07-16 · feature: tv-beta-polish · draft · round 0

TL;DR

First beta session surfaced 7 usability complaints, all on the TV screen (/room/:code): the lobby scrolls instead of fitting the TV, the playing layout wastes video space, the "you're up" overlay is too subtle and too short, TV-side play/skip buttons are redundant with the phone host controls, and the sounds are too timid. This plan restructures both TV states into fit-the-viewport horizontal layouts, makes the singer announcement a 5-second full-opacity moment, gamifies the sound effects, and removes playback buttons from the TV.

Context

Feature feat-008 ui-overhaul shipped 2026-07-16 (plan plans/karaoke-ui-overhaul.html, reviewed round 1; PR #3 squash-merged as cb1ee80). The 2026-07-16 checkpoint records all gates green: 401 unit tests, verification 11/11 with 16 screenshots. This plan is the follow-up polish pass driven by the first real beta session on a TV. No feature is currently in-progress, so tv-beta-polish can start clean.

Relevant history: the feat-008 verifier found a regression where hiding the TV lobby play button made it impossible to cold-start the party from the TV. That matters again here — the beta feedback asks to remove TV play/skip entirely. The phone host controls (app/components/join/controls-tab.tsx, host-gated via isHost on /join/:code) already carry play/pause/skip/volume, so the phone path exists; decision 1 below settles how far the TV removal goes.

Big picture

Both TV states become two-column, viewport-locked layouts. Nothing changes in the Durable Object, WebSocket protocol, or phone guest flow (except that the phone Controls tab becomes the only playback surface).

Decisions needed

1. How far do we remove play/skip from the TV? affects cold-start

Beta feedback: "hide play and skip on the main screen, leave it as host controls on the phone." The phone Controls tab already does everything — but if the host never opens their phone, a TV with zero controls cannot start the first song (the exact regression the feat-008 verifier caught).

OptionProsCons
Exactly what the beta feedback asked; cleanest TV Host must join from phone to start playback; lobby needs a "control from your phone" hint
TV can still cold-start the party solo Partial removal — TV still shows a control the feedback wanted gone

2. How do we gamify the sound effects?

Today: two ~100ms single-note synth blips (join 660Hz sine, add 880Hz triangle) at low gain (0.12 peak) in app/lib/party-sounds.ts — beta users barely noticed them. The synth engine is injectable and has 13 unit tests.

OptionProsCons
No assets to license/load; keeps injectable-ctx test pattern; adds a fanfare synced to the 5s overlay Synth character — chiptune-ish, not sampled "game show" audio
Full-fidelity game-show feel Asset sourcing/licensing, bundle weight, rework of the tested synth engine

3. Sub-agent model split for the build phases?

You asked for sonnet + opus delegation. The playing-layout restructure (phase 2) is the widest blast radius (5 components + the route grid); the rest are bounded edits.

OptionProsCons
Spends the stronger model on the phase most likely to break responsive behavior Slightly slower/pricier on phase 2
Cheapest; matches feat-008 (all sonnet builders) which worked Layout phase gets less capable first pass

Plan of record

All phases carry the fixed feedback specs: "you're up" overlay at 100% opacity, 5 seconds, animated exit; song title smaller with wrapping; queue heading smaller text and narrower rail; video as large as possible.

  1. Phase 1 — lobby goes horizontal. Two-column lobby: QR hero fixed on the left, joined-guest roster fills the right (grid of avatar chips, internal scroll only past ~20 guests). Acceptance: at 1920×1080 the lobby renders with zero page scroll; roster chips keep their entrance animation.
  2. Phase 2 — playing layout maximizes the video. Left column: video player at max size + song title below it (title drops from 56px to ~36px, wraps up to 2 lines). Right rail (~300–320px): participants strip on top, queue in the middle (heading shrinks from 36px to ~22px, internal scroll), QR card pinned at the bottom. TV play/skip removed per decision 1. Acceptance: at 1920×1080 no page scroll, video is the largest element on screen, a 90-char title wraps without clipping.
  3. Phase 3 — "you're up" moment. NowUpOverlay backdrop goes fully opaque (no translucency), dismiss timer 2.5s → 5s, and a proper exit animation (fade/scale-out) replaces the hard unmount. Reduced-motion gate stays. Acceptance: overlay is unreadable-proof (nothing bleeds through), visible ~5s, animates out.
  4. Phase 4 — gamified sounds. Per decision 2: join/add become multi-note jingles at higher gain, plus a "your turn" fanfare fired with the overlay. Mute toggle and injectable-ctx unit tests extended, not bypassed. Acceptance: existing sound tests green plus new ones; sounds audibly distinct across a room.
  5. Phase 5 — verify + ship. feature-verifier browser walk at TV viewport (golden path + error path, screenshots), verify-done gates, ship tv-beta-polish with evidence, PR to main. Acceptance: verification doc PASS, harness check green.
