Party screen rethink — the playing state as a cinema

2026-07-24 · feature: party-screen-cinema (feat-014) · draft · round 0

TL;DR

The playing screen currently spends 128px of vertical space on a two-line raw YouTube title, floats an empty queue in ~250px of void, and on the light theme puts a hard-black video box on cream paper. This plan treats the playing state as what it actually is — a cinema: the video is the only hero, everything else is instrumentation — with a cleaned single-line now-playing bar, a rail that reads as one instrument panel, a real type scale, and a decision about whether this screen should ever be light at all.

Context

Checkpoint 2026-07-24: feat-013 layout-evolution shipped (PR #13) — that pass unified the party screen's 24/32px rhythm, content-sized the roster board, and folded the playing top bar into the rail. It did not touch type, the now-playing block, the rail's internal composition, or the light theme (feat-012 policy: light shipped token-level, QA'd dark-only).

Decisions needed

1. Should the playing screen ever be light? This is the ugliest thing in your screenshot: a black-bordered video box sitting on cream paper, with the brass singer credit going mustard.

OptionProsCons
Kills the clash outright; the video always looks intentional; brass/pink stay on their tuned dark values A host who deliberately picked light gets a dark screen mid-song (arguably the point)

Warm paper frame around the video, no hard black box, credit color retuned for cream		 Honors the theme choice everywhere YouTube's own player chrome is black — you can style around the frame but not inside it; more work for a surface that fights its content
Smallest diff The thing you screenshotted stays broken

2. Where does the now-playing metadata go? Today: a 36px two-line title plus a brass credit line, stacked above the video, costing 128px.

OptionProsCons
Singer stays big and adjacent to the video (it's the social payload); recovers ~64px; no overlay risk over lyrics Very long titles must truncate rather than wrap (mitigated by decision 3)

Rail gets a "Now singing" card on top; video owns the whole main column		 Maximum video height (~95%) Buries the singer's name in a 311px column — the one thing the room looks up for
Zero permanent chrome Karaoke videos put lyrics near the top; an overlay competes with the words people are singing

3. Do we clean the YouTube title? Real strings from this repo's own D1 right now: Teenage Dirtbag - Wheatus | Karaoke Version | KaraFun, DANCING QUEEN -  ABBA (HD Karaoke), PSY - GANGNAM STYLE(강남스타일) M/V, Darude - Sandstorm, Bohemian Rhapsody. Half of each string is noise on a TV.

Correction to my own first draft: I originally wrote "split on the first hyphen, left side is the artist" — checking the real rows, that would have mangled the exact song in your screenshot. Karaoke channels publish Song - Artist (Teenage Dirtbag - Wheatus) while official channels publish Artist - Song (Darude - Sandstorm). Fortunately the queue payload already carries channel, so the rule below decides the direction from data instead of guessing: if the left segment matches the channel name, the left side is the artist; otherwise assume the karaoke convention. Verified by hand against all five strings above.

Safer heuristics Still renders Artist - Song as one long line with no hierarchy

Type scale for the playing screen

Everything primary stays ≥24px per the 10-foot rule; the rail's documented 16px exception for cramped queue rows stands. Strike anything you disagree with.

  • Song title: 36px → 28px Fraunces 600, single line, truncate (the cleaned title fits — measured against the two real examples).
  • "NOW SINGING" eyebrow: 18px mono, uppercase, 0.18em tracking, muted — the label that makes the 28px title read as a title, not a heading.
  • Singer: brass italic text → an avatar chip (32px avatar + 24px name) on the same row as the title. Brass stays as the name's color, so the credit keeps its punctuation role.
  • Artist/source (from the title split): 20px muted, right after the song title with a middot separator — dropped entirely if the split found nothing.
  • Rail zone labels ("UP NEXT", guest count): 20px mono uppercase 0.14em tracking — one consistent label voice for all three zones, replacing today's mix of tv-title-sm serif and 18px semibold.
  • Queue rows: keep the 16px 2-line clamp (documented beta exception) but bump the singer line to 15px and the thumbnail to 56px so a row reads at a glance from the couch.
  • Room code on the ticket stub: keep code-marquee mono but drop to 20px — it's a persistent reference, not the lobby hero.

Spacing & framing fixes

  • Now-playing bar → video gap: 16px, and the bar itself gets no bottom border — the video's own top edge is the divider.
  • Drop the extra rounded black box around the player: the video sits flush in its flex box with a hairline ring, so on any theme there's no second black frame competing with YouTube's own chrome.
  • Balance the page frame: top and bottom padding equal (48px at 1080p) so the video is optically centered instead of 48px top / 45px bottom by accident.
  • Rail: give the panel a 16px inset from the screen edge instead of the current 4px, so it stops looking lopsided against the 80px left margin (still far tighter than the old dead 80px gutter).
  • Rail width 336px → 380px at ≥1600px viewports (queue rows are the cramped element; the extra 44px buys a readable title line) and stays 336px below that.
  • Empty queue state: anchored 24px under the UP NEXT label (not vertically centered in the leftover space), with a quiet "scan the code to add songs" line pointing at the ticket stub below.
  • Guest header band: avatar cluster (overlapping, up to 6 + "+N") replacing the single-chip row, so the band is worth its 64px.
  • Keep the two-line reserve OFF now that the title is one line — the video no longer needs to absorb a wrap, so it gets that height permanently.

Plan of record

  1. Phase 1 — app/lib/song-title.ts: pure cleanSongTitle helper + unit tests (real-world strings from the seed/verification runs as fixtures).
  2. Phase 2 — Now-playing bar: rebuild NowSingingBanner's tv variant as the single-line eyebrow + title + singer chip, wired to the helper.
  3. Phase 3 — Rail as one panel: header band with avatar cluster, UP NEXT zone with anchored empty state, ticket stub; dividers + 24px rhythm.
  4. Phase 4 — Theater surface + framing: pin the playing state to the dark surface (per decision 1), drop the extra black player box, balance page padding, rail inset/width.
  5. Phase 5 — Verify: measured re-capture at 1512×900 and 1920×1080 in both themes (video ≥85% of viewport, zero rail void, one-line title), feature-verifier walk, verify-done, ship + PR.

Scope & guardrails

  • In scope: the playing state of /room/:code and the components it owns (now-singing banner, queue rail TV variant, roster strip compact, join ticket sm).
  • Inherited, not redesigned: the lobby uses the same rail children, so it gets the type/label changes for free — but its two-column structure is untouched.
  • Out of scope: phone join tabs, dashboard, landing, admin, auth.
  • Frozen: every data-testid, all i18n keys (new strings land en+zh together), the ≥24px 10-foot rule for primary text, no gradient controls, brass never interactive.
  • Cleaned titles are display-only — the raw title stays the stored/queue/history value, so nothing downstream (history, search cache, phone tabs) changes meaning.
